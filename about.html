About VOLTRIX
Voltrix is an online earning platform that allows members to earn rewards by participating in phone-charging activities and completing assigned tasks.
The concept is designed around active participation: members engage in activities available through the platform, complete their daily tasks, and receive rewards according to the platform's earning structure.
Our Background
Voltrix was founded in the United States in 2006 and is fully licensed and registered. The company was established with the aim of providing people with opportunities to generate income.
Voltrix partners with Apple and Samsung for phone-charging center activities involving their devices, with a potential Tesla partnership planned for 2027.
With a branch in Los Angeles, California, Voltrix is expanding into Nigeria under the leadership of Regional Manager Shirley.

Task-Based Earning System
One of the main features of Voltrix is its task-based earning system. Members are expected to participate actively by completing the activities assigned to them each day.
The rewards available to members are linked to the platform's task structure and the applicable earning plan.
VIP Plans & Revenue
Voltrix offers different VIP plans, each with its own stated earning structure. Individuals who wish to become recognized participants must select and purchase a VIP plan.

⚠️ Members should carefully review the cost, task requirements, reward conditions, withdrawal rules, and other terms associated with each plan before making a financial commitment.

Referral Rebate System
Members are assigned different VIP levels, and each level has a corresponding daily referral rebate distributed across three levels.
Level A: Direct Referral — 5% Commission
Level B: Second-Level Referral — 3% Commission
Level C: Third-Level Referral — 1% Commission

📌 The exact amount earned from an individual referral depends on the company's approved rules, qualifying activity, and calculation method.
