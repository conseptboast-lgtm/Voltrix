❤️ Our Mission

At VOLTRIX, we believe that true success is measured by the lives we touch. A portion of our platform's proceeds goes directly into supporting widows, orphans, and the less privileged in our communities.

Every time you participate on VOLTRIX, you are indirectly contributing to a child's education, a widow's feeding, or a family's shelter.

🤝 How You Can Help

If you wish to make a direct donation to our charity fund, you can do so through the deposit page. 100% of your donation goes to the less privileged.
